Put your reference images here.

Filenames must match the "reference" field in config/scenes.json:

  khatushyam.jpg   - Khatu Shyam ji
  mahavir.jpg      - Bhagwan Mahavir
  celebrity.jpg    - the celebrity (needs their permission for commercial use)
  mascot.png       - brand mascot

Guidelines for a good reference image:
  - high resolution (1000px+ on the short side)
  - the subject front-facing and fully visible
  - clean, uncluttered background
  - no text, watermark or border burned into the image
  - for deities: correct iconography, since the model copies what it sees
